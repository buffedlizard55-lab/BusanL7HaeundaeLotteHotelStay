🚇 Transit Cards & Navigation in Busan

  • T-Money / WOWPASS: Purchase at any convenience store (CU, GS25, 7-Eleven at Haeundae Station). Works seamlessly on all Busan metro lines, buses, and taxis.
  • Naver Map / KakaoMap: Google Maps does not provide walking routes in Korea. Download Naver Map or KakaoMap (English supported) before landing.
  • Subway Fare: ₩1,400 per single adult ride with T-Money card. Free transfer between bus and metro within 30 minutes.
  • Haeundae Station: Busan Metro Line 2, Station #203. Exit 5 and 7 have escalators and elevators towards L7 Haeundae.

🍂 November Weather & Packing Advice

  • Temperatures: Mid-November averages 8°C (morning/evening) to 16°C (sunny midday).
  • Sea Breeze: Haeundae Beach, Songdo, and Oryukdo can feel chilly with coastal wind. Pack a medium coat, windbreaker, or light packable down jacket.
  • Footwear: Coastal trails (Dongbaekseom, Igidae) and market cobblestones (Gukje, Jagalchi) require sturdy walking shoes or sneakers.
  • Power Adapter: South Korea uses Type C & F plugs (220V, 60Hz).
